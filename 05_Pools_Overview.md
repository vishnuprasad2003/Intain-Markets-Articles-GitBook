# Pools Overview

## Overview

Pools are collections of loans grouped together for securitization, whole loan sales, or other structured finance transactions. They serve as the fundamental building blocks for presenting loan portfolios to investors, structuring deals, and managing transactions from creation through completion. Understanding pools helps you effectively organize loans and navigate the structured finance workflow.

## How the Platform Is Designed

The platform is designed around the concept that loans need to be organized and presented effectively to potential investors and deal participants. Pools provide a structured way to group loans, calculate aggregate characteristics, and manage the transaction process. When you create a pool, you're creating a container that automatically calculates metrics from the loans you include, making it easier to present opportunities and track transaction progress.

The platform recognizes that pools are collaborative entities—they start private to you, become shared for review, progress through structuring, and finalize as completed deals. Each stage has specific purposes and allows different types of actions, ensuring that pools progress correctly through the structured finance workflow with appropriate reviews and approvals at each step.

## What This Enables for Users

For **Issuers**, pools enable you to organize your loan portfolios effectively, present opportunities to potential investors and market makers, calculate aggregate metrics automatically, collaborate through feedback and approvals, and manage transactions from creation to completion—all while maintaining control over your data and visibility into the process.

For **Market Makers**, pools provide opportunities to review loan portfolios, assess structuring potential, accept mandates to structure deals, provide feedback and request changes, and facilitate transactions—helping you add value through structuring and coordination.

For **Investors**, pools offer visibility into investment opportunities, the ability to analyze pool characteristics and loan details, tools to evaluate risk and return profiles, and tracking of investment opportunities—enabling informed decision-making and efficient capital deployment.

For **Rating Agencies**, pools provide access to loan portfolios for analysis, tools to review pool characteristics and metrics, and data needed for rating purposes—enabling thorough evaluation while maintaining appropriate access controls.

## Key Principles to Understand

**Automatic Metric Calculation** - Pool metrics calculate automatically from mapped loans. You don't need to calculate them manually—total balance, loan count, weighted averages, and other statistics update automatically when loans are added or removed.

**One Pool Per Loan** - Loans can only belong to one pool at a time. If you want to move a loan to a different pool, you must unmap it from the current pool first. This ensures clear ownership and prevents conflicts.

**Status-Based Progression** - Pools progress through statuses from creation to deal completion. Each status represents a specific stage and determines what actions are available, ensuring proper workflow progression.

**Collaborative by Design** - Pools are designed for collaboration. You can share them with multiple parties simultaneously, receive feedback, make changes, and work together through the structuring process.

**Removed Loans Are Excluded** - Removed loans are excluded from pool calculations but remain visible for tracking. This allows you to maintain pool quality while preserving complete records.

**Complete Audit Trail** - All pool changes, status updates, and actions are recorded with who did what and when, ensuring complete transparency and accountability.

Understanding pools helps you effectively organize loans, present opportunities to investors, collaborate with other parties, and manage structured finance transactions from start to finish.
