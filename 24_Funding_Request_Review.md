# Funding Request Review

## Overview

When a borrower submits a funding request, the facility agent reviews it and approves it, rejects it, or asks for changes. You do not sign the funding request to approve it. This guide explains that review for facility agents.

## Who Can Use This

- **Facility Agents**: Review funding requests and make approval decisions

## When This Is Used

Use funding request review when:
- A funding request shows **In review (facility agent)**
- You need to check a borrower's drawdown
- You are ready to approve, reject, or ask for changes

## Step-by-Step Process

### Step 1: Access the Funding Request

1. **Navigate to Credit Facility**
   - Log in as a facility agent
   - From the left menu, click **Credit Facility**
   - Open the **Active Facilities** tab

2. **Find the Funding Request**
   - Open the master commitment that holds the request
   - The status shows **In review (facility agent)**
   - The action column shows **Review Funding Request**

3. **Click Review Funding Request**
   - Click **Review Funding Request** to open the review window

![Review Funding Request - Review](images/24-funding-request-review/review_funding_request_review.png)

### Step 2: Evaluate the Request

1. **Review Request Details**
   - Check the draw amount
   - Check the funding date
   - Read the purpose of funds
   - Check the draw currency

2. **Review Documentation**
   - Open the collateral addendum
   - Confirm the documents are complete

3. **Verify Capacity**
   - Confirm the draw amount fits the borrowing capacity that is still available
   - Check how much of the facility is already in use

### Step 3: Make Your Decision

The review window has three buttons.

#### Option 1: Approve

**When to approve:**
- The request meets the facility terms
- The draw amount is within capacity
- The documents are complete
- You have no compliance concern

**What happens:**
- Click **Approve**
- The status changes to **Approved**
- A funding notice is created. Its status is **Pending token generation**.
- Continue with the funding notice

You do not sign anything to approve the funding request.

#### Option 2: Reject

**When to reject:**
- The request does not meet the facility terms
- There is not enough capacity
- The documents are not acceptable
- There is a compliance problem

**What happens:**
- Click **Reject**
- Enter a rejection reason
- The status changes to **Rejected**. This is final.
- The borrower must create a new request

#### Option 3: Request Changes

**When to request changes:**
- A smaller issue can be fixed
- You need another document
- You need a clearer purpose, date, or amount

**What happens:**
- Click **Request Changes**
- Describe the changes you need
- The status changes to **Changes Requested**
- The borrower can edit the request and submit it again

## What Happens After Approval

After you approve a funding request:

1. **Funding notice created**
   - The notice starts as **Pending token generation**

2. **Approve the funding notice**
   - Click **Approve** on the funding notice

3. **Sign for each lender**
   - The action shows **E-sign (0/n)**
   - Sign for each lender in Adobe Sign
   - The count moves forward as you sign, such as 1/n and 2/n

4. **Lender visibility**
   - A lender can see the funding notice after the signature for that lender is complete

5. **Lender fund transfer**
   - The lender reviews the notice, sends the funds, and clicks **Confirm and Settle**

## Rules & Validations

- **Review only while it is in review**: The review actions are available when the status is **In review (facility agent)**.

- **No signature for this approval**: Funding request approval does not use an electronic signature. Signatures apply later, on the funding notice.

- **Rejected is final**: A rejected request cannot be edited or sent again. The borrower creates a new request.

- **The notice is created for you**: Approval creates the funding notice. You do not create it as a separate form.

## Decision Summary

| Decision | Status Change | Funding Notice | Next Steps |
|----------|---------------|----------------|------------|
| Approve | **Approved** | Created automatically | Process the funding notice and sign for each lender |
| Reject | **Rejected** | Not created | Borrower creates a new request |
| Request Changes | **Changes Requested** | Not created | Borrower edits and resubmits |
