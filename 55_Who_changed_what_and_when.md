# Who Changed What and When

## Overview

The platform tracks every change with complete details about who made the change, what changed, and when it happened. This guide explains how to view and understand this change history, helping you track item progression, maintain accountability, and understand the complete history of any item. All changes are automatically recorded with attribution and timestamps, ensuring complete traceability and accountability throughout the platform.

## Reference Details

**Status History** - Status history shows all status changes with complete details. Every time an item's status changes, it's recorded with who changed it, when it changed, what the previous status was, what the new status is, and any reason or comments explaining the change. All status changes are shown in chronological order, creating a complete progression history. You can see how items moved through their workflows and understand the sequence of status changes.

**Action History** - Action history shows all actions taken on items. Every action is recorded with what action was taken (create, update, approve, reject, etc.), who performed the action, when the action occurred, what was affected, what the result was, and any additional details about the action. All actions are shown in chronological order, creating a complete action log. You can see all actions taken on items and understand what happened and when.

**Document History** - Document history tracks document-related activities. Document uploads are recorded with when documents were uploaded and by whom. Document changes are recorded with any modifications made. Previous versions of documents are saved, maintaining complete version history. Access tracking may show who accessed documents and when. This history helps you track document-related activities and understand document progression.

**Change Attribution** - Every change is attributed to specific users. You can see who made each change, when they made it, and what changed. This creates complete accountability for all activities and ensures transparency in the platform. User attribution ensures that all changes are traceable to specific individuals, supporting accountability and compliance.

**Timestamp Accuracy** - All timestamps are accurate and consistent, using standardized time formats. This ensures that you can accurately determine when events occurred and in what order. Timestamps are essential for audit trails and compliance purposes. Accurate timestamps help you understand the sequence of events and track progression over time.

**Viewing Change History** - You can view change history by navigating to an item's detail page and looking for history, audit trail, or activity sections. Change history is typically organized chronologically, showing the most recent changes first or in chronological order. You can see complete details about each change, including who made it, when it happened, and what changed. This access helps you understand item progression and track activities.

**Field-Level Changes** - Some systems track field-level changes, showing which specific fields were modified. This provides detailed information about what changed within items. Field-level tracking helps you understand exactly what was modified and how items evolved over time.

**Comment History** - Comments and notes are tracked with who made them and when. This helps you understand the context of changes and decisions. Comment history provides additional context for understanding why changes were made and how decisions were reached.

**User Attribution Is Complete** - Every change is attributed to the specific user who made it. This ensures accountability and enables you to see who was responsible for specific actions or decisions. Complete attribution supports accountability and helps you understand who was involved in item progression.

**Multiple History Types** - Different types of history are tracked—status history, action history, document history, etc. Understanding these different types helps you find the information you need. Multiple history types provide comprehensive tracking of all activities.
