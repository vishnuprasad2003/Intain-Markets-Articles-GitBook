# Reporting and Document Management

This article explains how to view, publish, and manage deal reports and documents generated after the monthly deal cycle in the Intain Admin platform.

## Overview

The **Reporting** module serves as the primary hub for reviewing the results of the monthly deal cycle. Once recurring calculations are completed, the platform generates a set of standard reports that detail collateral performance, loan-level data, and deal-level summaries.

These reports provide transparency for all stakeholders and are securely stored on the platform for ongoing access and audit.

## Who Can Access Reporting

* **Trustee**: Can view, download, and publish reports to investors
* **Investor**: Can view and download published reports only

Investors cannot publish or modify reports.

## Standard Reports

Reports are accessed through the **Reporting** sidebar menu. The following standard reports are available for each payment period:

### Collateral Report

The Collateral Report displays detailed performance metrics for the loan pool for the selected payment period. It reflects the results of loan processing and recurring calculations.

This report is typically reviewed by the Trustee before being shared with investors.

![Collateral report view](../.gitbook/assets/collateral-report.png)

### Loan Tape

The Loan Tape report provides access to the standardized monthly loan tape generated during loan processing.

* Displays loan-level data in the Intain standard format
* Available for download in **Excel** format
* Useful for offline analysis and reconciliation

![Loan tape report view](../.gitbook/assets/loan-tape-report.png)

### Consolidated Report

The Consolidated Report provides a high-level summary of the entire deal for the selected payment period.

* Combines key collateral and deal-level information
* Available for download in **PDF** or **Excel** formats
* Commonly used for investor reporting and internal review

![Consolidated report view](../.gitbook/assets/consolidated-report.png)

## Publishing Reports to Investors

After verifying that reports are accurate, the Trustee can make them available to investors.

### Publishing Reports

* Click **Publish** from the Reporting module
* The Collateral Report, Loan Tape, and Consolidated Report are shared with investors assigned during deal setup

Once published, reports become visible in the Investor portal.

## Managing Deal Files

The **Files** section allows you to maintain a centralized digital repository of documents related to the deal.

### Adding Documents

Trustees can upload various document types, including:

* Original Deal Documents
* Disclosures
* Servicer reports
* Supporting files related to the transaction

### Accessing Documents

* Authorized users can view and download uploaded documents at any time
* Investors have **view-only access** to documents that are shared with them
* All files remain securely stored on the platform for reference

## Important Notes

* Reports are generated **per payment period**
* Only Trustees can publish reports to investors
* Investors cannot modify or publish reports
* Published reports represent finalized outputs of the deal cycle
