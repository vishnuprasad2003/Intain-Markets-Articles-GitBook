# New Features

**Last updated: September 2026**

---

### Asset Sale *(February – September 2026)*

Full asset sale workflow is live: create a deal, send for underwriter review, take investor commitments, settle with NFT transfer, receive repayment, and burn NFTs when the deal closes. Analytics (Asset Analysis, Risk Surveillance, Reports) are available inside each deal.

---

### Pool Analysis via IDA *(September 2026)*

Underwriters, facility agents, and investors can open IDA analytics directly on a pool preview.

---

### Delegation *(2026)*

Send a task — such as field mapping or deal setup — to an admin to complete on your behalf.

---

### Activity Audit *(March 2026)*

A single activity log for the entire platform. Open **Activity Audit** in the sidebar to filter, sort, and export.

---

### Sign in with Microsoft *(September 2026)*

If your organisation uses Microsoft Entra (formerly Azure AD), click **Sign in with Microsoft** on the login page. Multi-role accounts choose a role after sign-in.

---

### View As — Admin Support *(February 2026)*

Admins can open the platform as another user in read-only mode for support purposes. No data is changed.
