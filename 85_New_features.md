# New Features

**Last updated: September 2026**

---

### Credit Facilities Module *(February – September 2026)*

Full credit facility workflow is live: term sheets, master commitments, funding requests, and funding notices. Borrowers propose terms, the facility agent reviews and sets up the facility, and lenders approve and fund draws.

---

### Asset Sale *(February – September 2026)*

Create a deal, send for underwriter review, take investor commitments, settle with NFT transfer, receive repayment, and burn NFTs when the deal closes. Analytics (Asset Analysis, Risk Surveillance, Reports) are included inside each deal.

---

### Credit Facility Deal Setup Wizard *(September 2026)*

Facility agents configure a new deal in a guided wizard: General, Facilities, Fees, Expenses, Accounts, Triggers, Borrowing Base, Calculations, Waterfall, and Review.

---

### Pool Analysis via IDA *(September 2026)*

Market makers and investors can open IDA analytics directly on a pool preview.

---

### Participation Agreements *(2026)*

Participation Agreements appear in the sidebar alongside Asset Sale, Credit Facilities, and Securitization. Issuers, market makers, and investors use them for participation workflows.

---

### Delegation *(2026)*

Send a task — such as field mapping or deal setup — to an admin to complete on your behalf.

---

### Activity Audit *(March 2026)*

A single activity log for the entire platform. Open **Activity Audit** in the sidebar to filter, sort, and export.

---

### Sign in with Microsoft *(February 2026)*

If your organisation uses Microsoft Entra (formerly Azure AD), click **Sign in with Microsoft** on the login page. Multi-role accounts choose a role after sign-in.

---

### View As — Admin Support *(February 2026)*

Admins can open the platform as another user in read-only mode for support purposes. No data is changed.

---

### Deal Modelling Workbench *(January – February 2026)*

Market makers and investors can build cashflow models for a pool and compare scenarios.

---

### Notifications *(March 2026)*

The notification drawer updates in real time — new alerts appear without a page refresh.

---

### Search & Filter on Large Lists *(March – September 2026)*

Search and filter are available on batches, NFTs, pools, loan registry, and admin lists.
