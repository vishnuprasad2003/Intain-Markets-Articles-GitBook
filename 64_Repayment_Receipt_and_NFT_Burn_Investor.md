# Repayment Receipt & NFT Burn

## Overview

This guide provides investors with step-by-step instructions for confirming repayment receipt and burning receivables NFTs to close out their position in an asset sale deal. When the issuer initiates repayment, the investor reviews the repayment details, confirms receipt of the wire transfer, and burns the receivables NFT to finalize deal closure.

## Who Can Use This

- **Investors**: All steps in this guide are performed by the investor role

## When This Is Used

Use this guide when:
- The issuer has initiated repayment on an active asset sale deal
- You have received a wire transfer from the issuer for a repayment
- You need to confirm receipt and close out your investment position
- You need to burn your receivables NFT after repayment confirmation

## Step-by-Step Process

### Part 1: Confirm Repayment Receipt

#### Step 1: Access the Deal

1. Navigate to **Asset Sale** from the left sidebar menu
2. Locate the deal showing **Repayment In Progress** status
3. Click on the deal to open the deal details page

#### Step 2: Review Repayment Details

1. Navigate to **Investment Operations** within the deal details
2. Click **Confirm Repayment Receipt**
3. Review the repayment information:
   - **Repayment Amount**: The amount the issuer has transferred
   - **Repayment Type**: Full or partial repayment
   - **Repayment Date**: When the repayment was initiated
   - **Wire Confirmation**: The issuer's wire confirmation document
   - **Memo**: Any notes from the issuer

#### Step 3: Verify and Accept

1. Verify that the repayment amount matches the wire transfer you received
2. Click **Review & Confirm** to proceed
3. If the transferred amount is correct, click **Yes, Accept Repayment**
4. The repayment is confirmed and recorded on the platform

### Part 2: Burn the Receivables NFT

#### Step 4: Access Receivables

1. Navigate to **Asset Analysis** within the deal details
2. Click on the **Receivables** tab
3. Your receivables NFTs are listed with details

#### Step 5: Initiate the Burn

1. Click the **Burn** button next to the receivable NFT
2. A confirmation dialog appears with details of the NFT to be burned
3. Review the burn details carefully

#### Step 6: Confirm the Burn

1. Click **Yes, Burn NFT** to confirm
2. The platform submits the burn transaction to the blockchain
3. The NFT is permanently burned and removed from your wallet
4. The burn event is recorded with a blockchain transaction hash and timestamp

### Part 3: Deal Closure

#### Step 7: Verify Deal Closure

1. After the burn completes, the deal dashboard updates
2. The deal status changes to **Closed** showing **Fully Repaid** and **100% repaid**
3. All settlement, repayment, and burn events are preserved in the audit trail

#### Step 8: Review Audit Trail (Optional)

1. Navigate to **Settlement Details** within the deal
2. Click **View details** in the Settlement Activity section
3. The complete event trail shows every action from settlement through repayment and closure

## Rules & Validations

- Repayment confirmation is only available when the deal is in **Repayment In Progress** status
- You must verify the repayment amount against your actual wire receipt before accepting
- NFT burn is only available after you have confirmed repayment receipt
- Burning an NFT is irreversible — the position is permanently closed
- Only the investor holding the NFT can initiate the burn
- The deal reaches Closed status only after all investor NFTs are burned

## What Happens Next

After the deal is closed:
- The deal remains in your Asset Sale dashboard for reference and reporting
- The complete audit trail is permanently preserved
- Settlement Details provide full visibility into all events
- No further operational actions are available on a closed deal
