# Pool and Loan Review

## Who Can Use This

- **Market Makers** — review shared pools, provide feedback, request loan removal, share with investors
- **Investors** — review shared pools, provide feedback, request loan removal; cannot share further

## Accessing Shared Pools

**Pools** → your dashboard shows pools shared with your organisation. Click **Pool ID** to open pool details.

## What to Review

| Section | What you see |
|---|---|
| **Summary tiles** | Pool metrics |
| **Loans tab** | Individual loans; cross icon = request removal; chat icon = loan-level feedback |
| **Loan Tape** | Full field data; As Of Date dropdown; download XLSX/CSV (if permitted) |
| **Strats** | Loan characteristic distributions |
| **Performance** | Delinquency, default, prepayment data |
| **Documents** | Supporting materials from issuer |

## Actions

**Accept / Reject mandate** — from the Pools dashboard Actions column:
- **Accept** → status changes to **Under Review** (Preview) or **Deal** (Start Deal); feedback becomes available
- **Reject** → issuer is notified; can revise and re-share

**Pool-level feedback** — Feedback section → add comment.

**Loan-level feedback** — Loans tab → **chat box icon** on a loan.

**Request loan removal** — Loans tab → **cross icon** on a loan → issuer accepts (Removed) or rejects.

**Download** — Loan Tape section → Download (if issuer enabled download permission for your organisation).

**Share with investors** (market makers only) — **Share** button at top of pool details.

## Key Rules

- Market makers cannot leave feedback until mandate is accepted
- Download and feedback availability depends on permissions set by the issuer
- Investors cannot share pools further
- All feedback and decisions are recorded with timestamps
