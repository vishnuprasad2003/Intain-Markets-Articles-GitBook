# Term Sheet Review

As a facility agent, you review submitted term sheets and choose to Approve, Reject, or Request Changes.

## Steps

1. **Credit Facility → Set-up tab** → find term sheet with status **In review (facility agent)** → **Review Term Sheet**
2. Check:
   - Commitment amount, advance rate, pricing index + margin (or fixed rate), maturity date, drawdown frequency
   - Supporting documents: collateral profile, financial statements, KYC documents, collateral data
3. Choose your decision:

![Review Term Sheet - FA](<.gitbook/assets/review_term_sheet_FA (3).png>)

## Decisions

| Decision | When to use | Status changes to | What happens next |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Approve** | Terms acceptable, documents complete | Accepted | Master commitment created in Draft |
| **Reject** | Cannot be fixed by revision | Rejected (final) | Borrower must create new term sheet |
| **Request Changes** | Minor issues can be fixed | Changes Requested | Borrower edits, re-signs, resubmits |

- **Rejection is final** — the borrower cannot edit that term sheet; must start a new one
- **Request Changes** allows more than one round of revisions
- Write a clear reason when rejecting or requesting changes
