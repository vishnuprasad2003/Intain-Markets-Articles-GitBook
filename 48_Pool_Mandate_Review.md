# Pool Mandate Review

## Who Can Use This

Market Makers / Facility Agents — when a pool appears in your dashboard with **Mandate Pending** (Preview) or **Ready for Deal** (Start Deal) status.

## Review Process

1. **Pools dashboard** → find pool with **Mandate Pending** or **Ready for Deal**
2. Click the **Pool ID** (or **Review** in Actions) to open pool details
3. Review these sections:

| Section | What to check |
|---|---|
| **Summary tiles** | Pool metrics and composition |
| **Loans tab** | Individual loans; click Loan ID for detailed analytics |
| **Loan Tape** | Full loan-level data; use As Of Date dropdown; download XLSX/CSV if permitted |
| **Strats** | Loan distributions by characteristic |
| **Performance** | Delinquency, defaults, prepayment data |
| **Documents** | Offering memorandums and supporting materials |

## Making Decisions

**From the Pools dashboard:**

| Action | How | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Accept** | Click **Accept** → confirm popup | Preview: your view → **Under Review**; feedback and loan removal enabled. Start Deal: pool → **Deal** |
| **Reject** | Click **Reject** → confirm popup | Issuer notified; can revise and re-share |

![Submit Pool for Mandate](<.gitbook/assets/2.SubmitPoolForMandate (1).png>)

## After Accepting (Preview)

- **Feedback section** — add pool-level comments
- **Loans tab → chat box** — add loan-level feedback
- **Loans tab → cross icon** — request loan removal; issuer decides to accept (Removed) or reject
- **Share button** — share pool with investors

## Key Rules

- Feedback is only available after mandate acceptance
- You can share with investors but not with other market makers
- Accept and reject decisions are recorded with timestamps
