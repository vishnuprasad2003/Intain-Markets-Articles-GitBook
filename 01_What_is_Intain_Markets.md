# What is Intain Markets

## Overview

Intain Markets is a digital infrastructure platform for structured finance transactions. It provides transparency, efficiency, and security for all parties—issuers, borrowers, investors, lenders, market makers, facility agents, servicers, and rating agencies—through secure, workflow-driven processes. The platform manages coordination between parties, status transitions, approvals, and relationships, so you can focus on business decisions rather than transaction mechanics.

## Platform Design

Intain Markets is built on the principle that structured finance transactions require careful coordination between multiple parties with distinct roles. These transactions progress through defined stages, require approvals at critical points, and maintain transparency throughout their lifecycle.

The platform provides:

**Workflow-Driven Progression** - Transactions follow structured paths from creation to completion with clear stages and decision points. The platform guides you through proper sequences, preventing skipped stages and ensuring prerequisites are met.

**Status-Based Control** - Each item has a status indicating its position and available actions. Statuses actively control what's possible, ensure proper progression, and prevent improper steps.

**Quality Approval Gates** - Critical stages require review and approval from authorized parties. These checkpoints ensure items meet standards, comply with requirements, and have proper authorization.

**Role-Based Access** - Your role determines visibility, available actions, and responsibilities. The platform filters information and actions automatically based on your role.

**Multi-Party Collaboration** - Share items with multiple parties for parallel review. Issuers share pools with market makers and investors; borrowers submit term sheets to facility agents.

**Complete Traceability** - Every action, status change, and decision is recorded with attribution and timestamps, providing accountability, compliance support, and transparency.

## Capabilities by Role

**Issuers and Borrowers** - Organize loan portfolios into pools, present investment opportunities, submit credit facility requests, and manage transactions from creation to completion. Track submissions, respond to feedback, and see when approvals occur.

**Market Makers and Facility Agents** - Review opportunities, accept mandates, provide feedback, request changes, approve submissions, and facilitate transactions. Clear decision points ensure you review items at the appropriate stage.

**Investors and Lenders** - View investment opportunities, review pool characteristics and loan details, approve or reject funding requests, and track investments. Make independent decisions with complete information.

**Servicers and Paying Agents** - Access assigned pools and facilities, manage loan servicing and payment distributions, and track ongoing activities for smooth post-deal operations.

**Rating Agencies** - Read-only access to pool information for analysis and rating purposes with appropriate security controls.

## Key Principles to Understand

**Workflow-Driven Design** - The platform is built around workflows, not just features. Every action fits into a larger process, and the system guides you through the appropriate sequence of steps. Understanding workflows helps you know what needs to happen next and how your actions fit into the overall transaction process.

**Status-Based Control** - Statuses are not just labels—they control what actions are available, ensure proper progression, and prevent skipping important steps. Understanding statuses helps you know what to expect and what you can do at each stage. When actions are disabled, it's usually because the status doesn't allow them yet, or prerequisites haven't been met.

**Approval Gates** - Approvals are not obstacles but quality checkpoints. They ensure that items meet standards, comply with requirements, and have proper authorization before proceeding—protecting all parties involved. Understanding approvals helps you appreciate their value in ensuring quality and maintaining compliance.

**Role-Based Access** - Your role determines your view and capabilities. The platform automatically filters information and actions based on your role, showing you only what's relevant and appropriate for your responsibilities. Understanding your role helps you know what you can see and do, and what others can see and do.

**Collaborative by Design** - The platform is built for multi-party collaboration. Sharing, feedback, and parallel review are core capabilities, not add-ons. Multiple parties can work together efficiently without conflicts. Understanding collaboration helps you work effectively with other parties.

**Complete Traceability** - Every action is tracked, every status change is recorded, and every decision is documented. This traceability ensures accountability, supports compliance, and provides transparency for all parties. Understanding traceability helps you appreciate how the platform maintains accountability and supports compliance.

**Automatic Coordination** - The platform handles the complexity of coordinating multiple parties, managing status transitions, tracking approvals, and maintaining relationships—you focus on your business decisions, not the mechanics. Understanding this automation helps you appreciate how the platform simplifies complex processes.

**Security and Compliance** - Security is built into every aspect, from authentication and role-based permissions to digital signatures and audit trails. The platform ensures that transactions are protected and compliant. Understanding security and compliance helps you trust that your data and transactions are secure.

Understanding these principles helps you appreciate how Intain Markets brings structure, security, and efficiency to complex multi-party transactions while maintaining the flexibility and transparency needed for successful structured finance deals. These principles work together to create a platform that supports effective collaboration, ensures quality and compliance, and provides the transparency and accountability needed for structured finance transactions.
