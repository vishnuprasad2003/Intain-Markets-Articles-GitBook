# What is Intain Markets

[Intain Markets](https://intainft.com/intainmarkets) is a blockchain-based platform for managing structured finance transactions end-to-end — from loan onboarding through deal structuring, investor commitment, tokenized settlement, repayment, and closure.

## Four Product Lines

| Product Line | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Securitization** | Pool creation, deal structuring with tranches and waterfalls, investor distribution, ESMA reporting |
| **Credit Facilities** | Term sheets, master commitments, multi-lender funding requests, e-signed funding notices, settlement |
| **Asset Sale** | Whole loan sale from deal creation through commitment, blockchain settlement, repayment, and NFT burn |
| **Participation Agreements** | Shared ownership and risk distribution of loan assets |

## Roles

| Role | Works in |
|---|---|
| **Issuer / Borrower** | All — creates pools, loans, deals, term sheets |
| **Market Maker / Facility Agent** | Securitization, Credit Facilities — structures deals, approves requests |
| **Investor / Lender** | All — reviews, commits, settles, receives repayment |
| **Underwriter** | Asset Sale — approves deals before investors can commit |
| **Servicer** | Uploads loan tapes for assigned deals |
| **Paying Agent** | Manages payment distributions in securitization |
| **Rating Agency** | Read-only review and feedback on shared pools |
| **Admin** | Platform administration, KYC, delegation support |

## What the Platform Does

**Workflow enforcement** — Each transaction follows a defined status path. Buttons are only shown when your role and the item's status allow the action.

**Blockchain tokenization** — Loans and receivables are minted as NFTs. Settlement and repayment are recorded on-chain. Token transfers provide immutable ownership records.

**Multi-party collaboration** — Pools and deals are shared with specific organisations. Feedback, approvals, and e-signatures are tracked per participant.

**Audit trail** — Every action is logged with user identity and timestamp. Blockchain steps produce an on-chain reference verifiable outside the platform.

**ESMA reporting** — Automated Annex 2, 12, and 14 reports for European securitization compliance.
