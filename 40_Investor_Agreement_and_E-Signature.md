# Investor Agreement & E-Signature

## Overview

Investor agreements are legally binding documents that formalize the terms between the issuer and each investor in an asset sale deal. The Intain Markets platform supports electronic signature workflows via Adobe Sign, allowing agreements to be created, distributed, signed, and tracked entirely within the platform. This workflow covers the complete agreement process from document preparation to fully executed signatures.

## Workflow Overview

The investor agreement workflow ensures that all legal documentation is properly executed before settlement can proceed. Agreements are managed per investor, and each investor's signature status is tracked independently.

**Document Preparation** — The issuer prepares the sale agreement document and uploads it to the deal.

**Signature Routing** — The platform routes the document for electronic signature via Adobe Sign, creating individual signing requests for each investor.

**Investor Signing** — Each investor receives their agreement, reviews the terms, and signs electronically.

**Tracking and Verification** — The platform tracks each investor's signing status and stores the executed documents.

## Key Stages

### Stage 1: Agreement Document Upload

The issuer prepares the investor agreement document and uploads it to the deal. This typically happens during the deal preparation phase, before or during the commitment process.

- The issuer navigates to the deal details and accesses the agreement section
- The sale agreement document is uploaded to the platform
- The document becomes the template for all investor agreements in this deal

### Stage 2: Electronic Signature Initiation

Once the agreement document is uploaded and investors have committed to the deal, the electronic signature process begins.

- The platform creates individual signing requests via Adobe Sign for each investor
- Each investor receives a notification that their agreement is ready for signing
- The signing status for each investor is set to **Pending**

### Stage 3: Investor Review and Signing

Each investor reviews and signs their agreement independently.

- The investor accesses the deal and opens their agreement for review
- The agreement opens in the Adobe Sign interface embedded within the platform
- The investor reviews the terms and signs electronically
- Upon signing, the investor's agreement status updates to **Signed**

### Stage 4: Manual Agreement Upload (Alternative)

For cases where electronic signing is not used, the issuer can upload manually signed agreements.

- The issuer obtains physically signed agreements from investors
- Signed documents are uploaded to the platform for each investor
- The system records the manually uploaded agreement with the same tracking capabilities

### Stage 5: Agreement Completion

Once all investor agreements are signed or uploaded, the deal is ready to proceed to settlement.

- All investor agreements show **Signed** status
- The platform validates that all required agreements are in place
- The deal can proceed to the settlement phase

## How the Workflow Progresses

The agreement workflow runs in parallel with the commitment process. Agreements can be initiated after investor commitments are received and before settlement begins. The key gate is that all investor agreements must be fully executed before settlement can proceed.

Each investor's agreement is independent — one investor signing does not affect another's process. The deal-level status reflects the aggregate: all signed, some pending, or all pending.

## Important Points to Know

**Adobe Sign Integration** — The platform uses Adobe Sign as the electronic signature provider. Investors sign within the platform interface without needing a separate Adobe Sign account.

**Per-Investor Tracking** — Each investor has their own agreement instance with independent status tracking. The deal details page shows a summary of all investor agreement statuses.

**Pre-Settlement Gate** — Settlement cannot begin until all investor agreements are executed. This ensures legal documentation is complete before any financial transfer occurs.

**Document Storage** — Signed agreements are stored securely within the platform and linked to the deal for audit and compliance purposes.

**Dual Path Support** — The platform supports both electronic signing (Adobe Sign) and manual upload of pre-signed documents, providing flexibility for different operational preferences.

**Audit Trail** — Every signature event (sent, viewed, signed) is logged with timestamps, providing a complete audit trail for compliance and legal review.
