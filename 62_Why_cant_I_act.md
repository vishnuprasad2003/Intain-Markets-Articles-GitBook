# Why Can't I Act

## Overview

This comprehensive troubleshooting guide helps you understand why you can't take certain actions and how to resolve common issues preventing you from acting. Use this guide when actions are disabled or unavailable to diagnose and resolve problems.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How do I diagnose why I can't act?**

A: Check the button or action by hovering over disabled buttons to see tooltips, check status messages nearby, look for error or warning messages, review item status badges, and check requirements sections. Verify your role is correct, check if your role has permission, ensure you're assigned to the item, verify you have access, and confirm your role allows the action. Check the item status to ensure it allows the action, verify status is correct for workflow stage, and review status history if needed. Verify all required fields are filled, check if required documents are uploaded, ensure prerequisites are met, verify information is complete, and check validation messages. Systematic diagnosis helps identify issues.

**Q: Why can't I submit my term sheet?**

A: Possible causes include term sheet not being signed, electronic signature not completed, required fields not filled, required documents not uploaded, or validation errors present. Solutions include completing electronic signature, filling all required fields, uploading all required documents, fixing any validation errors, and ensuring the term sheet is complete. Completing all requirements enables submission.

**Q: Why can't I create a funding request?**

A: Possible causes include master commitment not being ACTIVE, facility not being operational, no available borrowing capacity, you don't have access to the facility, or facility status doesn't allow requests. Solutions include waiting for facility to become ACTIVE, checking facility status, verifying available borrowing capacity, ensuring you have access to the facility, and confirming facility status allows requests. ACTIVE facility status is required for funding request creation.

**Q: Why can't I approve a term sheet or funding request?**

A: Possible causes include item not being in review status, you're not the authorized reviewer, item is already approved or rejected, or you don't have permission for your role. Solutions include checking item status, verifying you're the authorized reviewer, ensuring item is in correct status for approval, and confirming your role has approval authority. Correct status and role are required for approval.

**Q: Why can't I edit an item?**

A: Possible causes include item not being in editable status, item is under review, item has been approved or rejected, or you don't have permission to edit. Solutions include checking item status, ensuring item is in Draft or CHANGES_REQUESTED status, verifying you have edit permission, and confirming status allows editing. Editable status is required for editing.

**Q: Why can't I see an item?**

A: Possible causes include item not being shared with you, you don't have a role in the item, item is in a status that restricts visibility, or you're using the wrong role. Solutions include checking if item is shared with your organization, verifying you have a role in the item, ensuring you're using the correct role, and checking item status for visibility restrictions. Proper sharing and role are required for visibility.

**Q: Why can't I sign a document?**

A: Possible causes include document not being ready for signing, you're not the authorized signer, signing process hasn't been initiated, or technical issues preventing signing. Solutions include checking document status, verifying you're the authorized signer, ensuring signing process has been initiated, and checking for technical issues. Proper authorization and process initiation are required for signing.

**Q: Why can't I view funding notice details?**

A: Possible causes include funding notice not being visible to your role, funding notice status restricts visibility, you don't have access permissions, or you're using the wrong role. Solutions include checking funding notice status, verifying your role has access, ensuring proper permissions, and confirming you're using the correct role. Proper access and permissions are required for viewing.

**Q: What should I do if I still can't act after checking everything?**

A: Review all diagnostic steps again to ensure nothing was missed. Check for any error messages or notifications that might provide additional information. Contact support or the item owner if you believe there's an issue. Verify your understanding of workflow requirements and role permissions. Be patient if you're waiting for other parties to act. Thorough review helps identify remaining issues.

**Q: How do I get help if I can't resolve the issue?**

A: Check platform help documentation, contact support with specific details about the issue, reach out to the item owner or administrator, review workflow documentation, and check for known issues or updates. Getting help ensures issues are resolved efficiently.

**Q: Can I see what actions are available for my role?**

A: Yes, enabled buttons show available actions, disabled buttons show unavailable actions with reasons, role documentation explains permissions, and status indicators show what's possible. Understanding available actions helps you know what you can do.

**Q: How do I know if I'm waiting for someone else?**

A: Check item status to see if it's waiting for another party, review status messages indicating pending actions, check workflow requirements for dependencies, and look for notifications about pending actions. Understanding dependencies helps you know what's needed.
