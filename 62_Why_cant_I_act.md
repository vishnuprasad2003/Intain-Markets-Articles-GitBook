# Why Can't I Act

## Overview

This troubleshooting guide helps you understand why you can't take certain actions and how to resolve common issues preventing you from acting. Use this guide when actions are disabled or unavailable to diagnose and resolve problems. Follow the diagnostic steps to identify and resolve issues.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How do I diagnose why I can't act?**

A: Check the button or action by hovering over disabled buttons to see tooltips, check status messages nearby, look for error or warning messages, review item status badges, and check requirements sections. Verify your role is correct, check if your role has permission, ensure you're assigned to the item, verify you have access, and confirm your role allows the action. Check the item status to ensure it allows the action, verify status is correct for workflow stage, and review status history if needed. Verify all required fields are filled, check if required documents are uploaded, ensure prerequisites are met, verify information is complete, and check validation messages.

**Q: Why can't I submit my term sheet?**

A: Possible causes include term sheet not being signed, electronic signature not completed, required fields not filled, required documents not uploaded, or validation errors present. Solutions include completing electronic signature, filling all required fields, uploading all required documents, fixing any validation errors, and ensuring the term sheet is complete.

**Q: Why can't I create a funding request?**

A: Possible causes include master commitment not being active, facility not being operational, no available borrowing capacity, you don't have access to the facility, or facility status doesn't allow requests. Solutions include waiting for facility to become active, checking facility status, verifying available borrowing capacity, ensuring you have access to the facility, and confirming facility status allows requests.

**Q: Why can't I approve a term sheet or funding request?**

A: Possible causes include item not being in review status, you're not the authorized reviewer, item is already approved or rejected, or you don't have permission for your role. Solutions include checking item status, verifying you're the authorized reviewer, ensuring item is in correct status for approval, and confirming your role has approval authority.

**Q: Why can't I edit an item?**

A: Possible causes include item not being in editable status, item is under review, item has been approved or rejected, or you don't have permission to edit. Solutions include checking item status, ensuring item is in Draft or CHANGES_REQUESTED status, verifying you have edit permission, and confirming status allows editing.

**Q: Why can't I see an item?**

A: Possible causes include item not being shared with you, you don't have a role in the item, item is in a status that restricts visibility, or you're using the wrong role. Solutions include checking if item is shared with your organization, verifying you have a role in the item, ensuring you're using the correct role, and checking item status for visibility restrictions.

**Q: What should I do if I still can't act after checking everything?**

A: Review all diagnostic steps again to ensure nothing was missed. Check for any error messages or notifications that might provide additional information. Contact support or the item owner if you believe there's an issue. Verify your understanding of workflow requirements and role permissions. Be patient if you're waiting for other parties to act.

Understanding why you can't act helps you diagnose issues effectively, resolve problems preventing actions, work within workflow requirements, and take appropriate actions when they become available.
