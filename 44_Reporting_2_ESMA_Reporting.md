# ESMA Reporting

This article explains how to access and download regulatory reports for **ESMA-compliant deals** in the Intain Admin platform.

## Overview

The **ESMA Reporting** module is a specialized section that becomes available only if a deal was marked as **ESMA Compliant** during the initial deal setup.

For eligible deals, the platform automatically generates regulatory reports required for European securitizations. These reports follow standardized ESMA formats and are prepared based on finalized loan processing and recurring calculations.

## Availability of ESMA Reporting

ESMA Reporting is available only when:

* The deal is explicitly configured as **ESMA Compliant**
* Loan Processing and Recurring calculations are completed for the selected payment period

If a deal is not marked as ESMA Compliant, this section remains inactive.

## ESMA Annexes

The platform supports the generation of the following ESMA Annexes:

### Annex 2 – Loan-Level Data

Annex 2 provides detailed loan-level information standardized for ESMA reporting requirements.

This annex includes:

* Individual loan attributes
* Performance and balance data
* Standardized field definitions aligned with ESMA templates

### Annex 12 – Investor Report

Annex 12 contains securitization-level and investor-focused information, including:

* Compliance test and trigger status
* Cash-flow and payment-related details
* Key deal performance indicators

This annex is commonly used for regulatory investor disclosures.

### Annex 14 – Inside Information

Annex 14 provides bond- and tranche-level details, including:

* Tranche and bond characteristics
* Account-level information
* Counterparty and structural data

This annex supports transparency requirements for inside information disclosures.

## Downloading ESMA Reports

Each ESMA Annex can be downloaded directly from the platform for the selected payment period.

### Available Download Formats

To support regulatory submissions and downstream systems, ESMA reports are available in the following formats:

* **Excel**
* **XML**
* **CSV**

Users can select the required format based on reporting or regulatory submission needs.

## Important Notes

* ESMA reports are generated **per payment period**
* Reports reflect finalized loan and deal calculations
* Only ESMA-compliant deals display this module
* Downloaded files represent official regulatory outputs for the period
