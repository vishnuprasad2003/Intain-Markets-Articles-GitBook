# Enabled vs Disabled Actions

## Overview

The platform automatically enables or disables actions based on your role, the item's status, and whether prerequisites are met. This guide explains the logic behind enabled and disabled buttons and actions.

## Roles Covered

This applies to all platform users who see enabled or disabled action buttons, regardless of role. The same logic applies to all roles, though specific actions vary by role. Whether you're an issuer, market maker, investor, facility agent, lender, or any other role, the principles of enabled and disabled actions are the same.

## What Each Role Can Do

All roles follow the same principles for enabled and disabled actions, though the specific actions available vary by role:

**When Actions Are Enabled** - Actions are enabled when you have permission, the status allows it, and all prerequisites are met. Enabled buttons mean you can take the action now.

**When Actions Are Disabled** - Actions are disabled when the status doesn't allow it, required information is missing, you're waiting for another party to act, the action was already completed, or you don't have permission. Disabled buttons show tooltips or messages explaining why they're disabled.

**How to Enable Actions** - To enable actions, ensure the item is in the correct status, complete all prerequisites (fill required fields, upload documents, complete previous steps), wait for other parties if needed, and verify your role allows the action.

**Common Scenarios** - Common scenarios include waiting for status changes, completing required fields, uploading required documents, waiting for other parties to act, ensuring you have the correct role, and verifying prerequisites are met.

## Important Access Notes

**Enabled Buttons Mean You Can Use Them** - If a button is enabled, you can click it and the action will proceed.

**Disabled Buttons Show Reasons** - Hover over disabled buttons or check tooltips to see why they're disabled. The platform provides explanations for disabled actions.

**Status Controls Availability** - The current status is the primary factor determining action availability. Status must allow the action for it to be enabled.

**Prerequisites Must Be Met** - Required information, documents, or previous steps must be complete before actions are enabled. Missing prerequisites disable actions.

**Permissions Determine Access** - Your role and permissions control what actions you can take. Even if status allows an action, you may not have permission.

**Workflow Order Matters** - Actions must happen in the correct order according to workflow rules. You cannot skip steps or proceed out of sequence.

**Complete Requirements** - All requirements must be met before actions are enabled. Partial completion keeps actions disabled.

**Check Messages** - The platform shows messages explaining why actions are disabled. Look for notifications or messages that explain restrictions.

**Status Changes Enable Actions** - When status changes, new actions may become available. Status progression enables new capabilities.

**Individual Tracking** - Each user's permissions and access are tracked individually. Your enabled/disabled actions reflect your specific role and access.

**Role and Status Together** - Both your role and the item's status determine what actions are available. You need both permission and appropriate status.

**Waiting for Others** - Some actions require other parties to act first. You may need to wait for approvals, submissions, or other actions before you can proceed.

**Previous Actions Required** - Some actions require previous actions to be completed first. You cannot skip steps or proceed without completing prerequisites.

**Document Requirements** - Some actions require documents to be uploaded or signed before they're enabled. Missing documents disable actions.

**Field Requirements** - Some actions require specific fields to be filled before they're enabled. Missing or incomplete fields disable actions.
