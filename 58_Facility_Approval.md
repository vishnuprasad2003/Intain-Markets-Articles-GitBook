# Facility Approval

## Who Can Use This

**Lenders** — when a master commitment appears in **Opportunities** with status **Pending Lender Approval**.

For multiple-branch facilities, you see only the sub-master commitment assigned to your organisation.

## Review and Approve

1. **Opportunities** → find master commitment with **Pending Lender Approval**
2. Click **Review & Approve** → review all sections in the popup:

| What to review | What to check |
|---|---|
| Commitment amount | Your organisation's specific capital commitment |
| Voting percentage | Your share of governance decisions |
| Advance rate | Share of collateral value the facility lends against |
| Interest rate | Fixed rate or pricing index + margin |
| Maturity date | When the facility expires |
| Drawdown frequency | How often the borrower can draw |
| Documents | Collateral profile, financial statements, KYC |

3. Click **Approve & E-Sign** → Adobe Sign / ZohoSign window opens → complete signature
4. Master commitment status → **Active** (if you are the first lender to approve)
5. Facility moves from Opportunities to your **Credit Facility** section

## Decision Options

| Option | Result |
|---|---|
| **Approve & E-Sign** | You are committed; facility activates if you are the first approver |
| **Do not approve** | Facility stays **Pending Lender Approval**; no action recorded for you |

> There is no formal "reject" action. The facility can proceed when any other lender approves.

## What Happens After Approval

- Facility agent completes **deal modelling** (Deal Setup)
- Borrower can **map loans** to the facility once deal modelling is complete
- Borrower creates **funding requests** to draw funds
- Facility agent approves requests → **funding notices** created automatically
- Funding notice e-signed by facility agent per lender → lender reviews, transfers funds, **Confirm and Settle**

## Key Rules

- E-signature (Adobe Sign or ZohoSign) is required — clicking Approve alone does not complete the process
- **Any one lender's approval** activates the master commitment — you do not wait for all lenders
- Approval represents a binding commitment to participate on the specified terms
- Facility must be in **Pending Lender Approval** status to approve
