# Facility Approval

## Overview

After a facility agent configures a master commitment and submits it for approval, lenders review the facility details and decide whether to commit. As a lender, you review the facility terms, your commitment amount, interest rates, and all supporting details, then approve by completing an electronic signature. Once any single lender approves and signs, the master commitment becomes active — you do not need to wait for all lenders.

This document explains what lenders see during the approval process, what to evaluate, and what happens after you approve.

## Who Can Use This

* **Lenders**: Review master commitments and make approval decisions. Each lender reviews and approves independently.

For facilities with a **multiple-branch** structure (where the main facility is divided into sub-facilities), each lender sees only the sub-master commitment assigned to their organization, not the full parent facility.

## When This Is Used

Use facility approval when:

* A new master commitment appears in your **Opportunities** section with status **PendingLenderApproval**
* You need to evaluate whether a facility's terms meet your investment criteria before committing capital
* You are ready to formally commit to a facility by completing your electronic signature
* You want to understand what your approval triggers for the broader facility workflow

The facility agent triggers this process by submitting the master commitment. When they do, the status changes from Draft to **PendingLenderApproval**, and every lender listed in the facility's lender groups can see it in their Opportunities section.

## Review Process

### Step 1: Access Pending Facilities

1. **Navigate to Opportunities**
   * Log in to the platform with your Lender credentials
   * From the left expandable menu, click on **Opportunities**
   * The Opportunities dashboard shows master commitments (or sub-master commitments) shared with your organization
2. **Find the Master Commitment**
   * Locate the facility awaiting your approval
   * Status shows **PendingLenderApproval**
   * For multiple-branch facilities, you see only the sub-master commitment assigned to you

### Step 2: Review the Facility

1. **Click Review & Approve**
   * In the Actions column, click **Review & Approve**
   * A popup opens showing comprehensive facility details
2. **Review All Sections**
   * Navigate through all tabs in the popup
   * Review basic facility information (facility name, type, structure)
   * Check commitment amounts and your participation
   * Verify interest rates (pricing index, margin, fixed rate) and fees
   * Review advance rate and maturity date
   * Examine drawdown frequency and facility rules
   * Review any attached documents
3. **Assess the Facility**
   * Evaluate whether the terms meet your investment criteria
   * Check your specific commitment amount and voting percentage
   * Review other participating lenders (if visible)
   * Ensure you understand all obligations before proceeding

### Step 3: Make Approval Decision

1. **Approve the Facility**
   * If satisfied with the facility terms, click **Approve & E-Sign**
   * An Adobe Sign (or ZohoSign) popup window opens automatically
   * Review the master commitment document
   * Complete the electronic signature process
2. **After Signing**
   * The master commitment (or your sub-master commitment) moves to your **Credit Facility** section
   * After any one lender approves and signs, the overall master commitment status changes to **Active**
   * The facility agent is notified
   * Facility setup can proceed

## Evaluation Criteria

When reviewing a master commitment, evaluate the following areas:

**Facility Terms**

* **Requested commitment amount** — The total facility size the borrower is seeking
* **Your commitment amount** — Your organization's specific capital commitment
* **Voting percentage** — Your share of governance decisions on the facility
* **Advance rate** — The percentage of collateral value the facility will lend against
* **Interest rate structure** — Whether the rate is fixed or floating (pricing index plus margin)
* **Maturity date** — When the facility expires
* **Drawdown frequency** — How often the borrower can draw funds

**Lender Commitments**

* Your voting percentage relative to other lenders
* Total facility size and your proportion
* Other participating lenders and their commitments (for single-branch facilities)

**Facility Rules**

* Borrowing limits and capacity calculations
* Collateral requirements and rules
* Any covenant compliance requirements

**Documentation**

* Completeness of supporting documents
* Collateral profile, financial statements, and KYC documents
* Whether the borrower's signed term sheet is available for reference

## Making Decisions

As a lender, you have two options:

| Decision    | Action                                              | Result                                                                                                |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Approve** | Click Approve & E-Sign and complete the e-signature | You are committed to the facility; master commitment activates if you are the first approver          |
| **Decline** | Do not approve                                      | The facility remains in PendingLenderApproval for other lenders; no action is recorded on your behalf |

There is no formal "reject" action for lender approval — you simply do not approve. The facility can proceed when any other lender approves.

## Rules & Validations

* **Review before approval**: You should thoroughly review all facility details before approving. Once approved, you are committed to the facility. Your approval represents a binding commitment to participate according to the specified terms.
* **E-signature required**: You must complete the electronic signature process (Adobe Sign or ZohoSign) to finalize your approval. Clicking "Approve" alone does not complete the process — the signed document is the formal commitment.
* **Any lender can activate**: Once any one lender approves and signs, the master commitment becomes **Active**. You do not need all lenders to approve before the facility can proceed. Each lender's approval is tracked individually with their own e-signature status.
* **Sub-facility assignment**: For multiple-branch facilities, you only see and approve the sub-master commitment assigned to your organization, not the parent facility. Your approval activates your sub-commitment independently.
* **Status progression is enforced**: The master commitment must be in **PendingLenderApproval** status for you to approve. You cannot approve a Draft commitment (it has not been submitted yet) or an Active commitment (it has already been approved).
* **Per-lender tracking**: Your approval, e-signature status, and timestamp are recorded against your specific lender entry in the facility's lender groups. Each lender's status is tracked independently — one lender's approval does not affect another's pending status.

## What Happens Next

**Immediately After Your Approval:**

* The master commitment status changes to **Active** (if you are the first lender to approve)
* Your facility moves from Opportunities to your **Credit Facility** section
* The facility agent receives notification that the facility is now active

**After the Facility Is Active:**

* The facility agent proceeds with **deal modelling** — configuring the financial structure, tranches, and allocation rules
* Once deal modelling is complete, the borrower can **map loans** to the facility as collateral
* The borrower can then create **funding requests** to draw funds against the facility

**When Funding Requests Are Created:**

* The facility agent reviews and approves funding requests
* Approved funding requests automatically generate **funding notices**
* The facility agent approves each funding notice and e-signs for each lender individually
* Once your e-signature is complete on a funding notice, it becomes visible to you in your Credit Facility section
* You review the drawdown details, transfer funds, and **Confirm and Settle** to complete the funding

**Your Ongoing Role:**

* Review funding notices as they arrive
* Select your payment method during funding notice review
* Transfer funds and confirm settlement for each drawdown
* Monitor facility utilization and remaining capacity
